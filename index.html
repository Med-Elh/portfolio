    SafeApps (eGrow) · 2025 - Present

    eGrow is a SaaS platform that lets businesses run and automate their operations from one place. It is built on the WhatsApp API and integrates with Shopify, delivery companies and other platforms, so a business manages everything in one tool instead of switching between several.

    That is where the technical part of my job sits. The product is APIs and integrations, so supporting it means working in them: I built the WhatsApp order confirmation and delivery tracking flows, the transactional email sequences, and the Meta Conversions API integrations that fixed attribution for our customers' ad campaigns.

    On top of that I own the full client lifecycle, from onboarding to renewal, and I close new business: 100+ sales in under 3 months. I run Meta ad campaigns (targeting, creative, conversion optimisation) and I script, film and edit UGC videos for the brand. Company revenue overall grew 15% in 2 months, driven mainly by the new sales I brought in through outreach.

    What I brought

    • Integrations built, not just configured
    • Client retention from day one
    • New revenue through sales
